# Acceptable Use Policy

## Purpose
Ensure responsible use of company IT resources to protect data integrity, availability, and confidentiality.

## Scope
Applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party users accessing company systems.

## Policy
- Do not share or reuse passwords.
- Access only data necessary for your role.
- Avoid installing unauthorized software.
- Report any suspicious activity to the Security Team.

## Enforcement
Violations may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.
