# Risk Register

This risk register identifies potential cybersecurity risks and their mitigation plans.

| ID  | Risk Description               | Likelihood | Impact | Score | Mitigation Plan                | Owner         |
|-----|--------------------------------|------------|--------|-------|--------------------------------|---------------|
| R1  | Phishing attacks on employees | High       | Medium | 3.0   | Security awareness training    | Security Team |
| R2  | Unauthorized access to data   | Medium     | High   | 3.5   | Role-based access control      | IT Manager    |
| R3  | Lost/stolen mobile device     | Medium     | Medium | 2.5   | Enable remote wipe and FDE     | IT Support    |
